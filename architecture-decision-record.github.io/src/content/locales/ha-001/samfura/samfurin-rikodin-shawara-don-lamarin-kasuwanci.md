# Samfurin rikodin shawara don lamarin kasuwanci

Wannan samfurin ADR yana jaddada ƙirƙirar lamarin kasuwanci don shawara, ciki har da ma’auni, ƴan takara, da kuɗaɗe.


## Matakin sama

* Take
* Matsayi
* Ma’aunin kimantawa
* Ƴan takarar da za a yi la’akari
* Bincike da nazari na kowane ɗan takara
  * Ya cika/bai cika ma’auni ba da dalili
  * Nazarin kuɗi
  * Nazarin SWOT
  * Ra’ayoyi da martani
* Shawara

## Zurfin bincike na matakin ƙasa

**Take**:

  * Gajeriyar jimla ta umarni a halin yanzu, ƙasa da haruffa 50, kamar saƙon commit na git.

**Matsayi**:

  * Ɗaya daga cikin an ba da shawara, an karɓa, an ƙi, an daina amfani, an maye gurbinsa, da sauransu.

**Ma’aunin kimantawa**:

  * Taƙaitawa: bayyana a taƙaice abin da muke neman ganowa da dalili.

  * Cikakkun bayanai

**Ƴan takarar da za a yi la’akari**:

  * Taƙaitawa: bayyana a taƙaice yadda muka gano ƴan takara, kuma ku jawo hankali ga duk wani na musamman.

  * Jera duk ƴan takara da zaɓuɓɓuka masu alaƙa; menene muke kimantawa a matsayin mafita masu yiwuwa?

  * Cikakkun bayanai

**Bincike da nazari na kowane ɗan takara**:

  * Taƙaitawa: bayyana a taƙaice hanyoyin bincike, kuma ku jawo hankali ga tsari, gungu, da na musamman.

  * Ya cika/bai cika ma’auni ba da dalili

    * Taƙaitawa

    * Cikakkun bayanai

  * Nazarin kuɗi

    * Taƙaitawa

    * Misalai

      * Lasisi, kamar yarjejeniyar kwangila da alƙawurran shari’a

      * Horo, kamar haɓaka ƙwarewa da sarrafa canji

      * Aiki, kamar tallafi da kulawa

      * Aunawa, kamar bandwidth da amfani da CPU

  * Nazarin SWOT

    * Taƙaitawa

    * Ƙarfi (Strengths)

    * Rauni (Weaknesses)

    * Dama (Opportunities)

    * Barazana (Threats)

  * Ra’ayoyin cikin gida da martani

    * Taƙaitawa

    * Misalai

      * Daga tawaga, a manufa wanda ainihin mutum ya rubuta

      * Daga sauran masu ruwa da tsaki

      * Sifofin inganci wato buƙatun giciye-aiki 

  * Ra’ayoyin waje da martani

    * Taƙaitawa

    * Wa ke ba da ra’ayin?

    * Waɗanne ƴan takara kuka yi la’akari?

    * Me kuke ƙirƙira? 

      * Misalai

        * B2B ko B2C

        * mai fuskantar waje ko na ma’aikata kawai

        * kwamfuta ko wayar hannu

        * gwaji ko samarwa

        * monolith ko microservices

    * Yaya kuka kimanta ƴan takara?

    * Me ya sa kuka zaɓi wanda ya yi nasara?

    * Me ke faruwa tun daga lokacin?

      * Misalai

        * Yaya wanda ya yi nasara ke aiki?

        * Kashi nawa cikin ɗari na zirga-zirgar masu amfani na zahiri a samarwa ke wucewa ta wanda ya yi nasara?

        * Waɗanne irin haɗe-haɗe ne ke ciki, kamar da bututun isar da ci gaba, tsarin sarrafa abun ciki, nazari da ma’auni, da sauransu?

        * Da abin da kuka sani yanzu, me za ku shawarci mutane su yi daban?

  * Labaran kai

**Shawara**:

  * Taƙaitawa

  * Cikakkun bayanai
