# Samfurin rikodin shawara mai amfani da Planguage

Duba https://www.iaria.org/conferences2012/filesICCGI12/Tutorial%20Specifying%20Effective%20Non-func.pdf

## Menene Planguage?

Planguage harshen tsarawa ne da ke amfani da waɗannan kalmomin maɓalli:

* Tag: Mai ganewa na musamman kuma mai dorewa
* Gist: Taƙaitaccen bayanin buƙata ko fannin da aka magance
* Requirement: Rubutun da ke bayyana buƙatar kanta
* Rationale: Dalilin da ke tabbatar da buƙatar
* Priority: Bayanin fifiko da da’awa kan albarkatu
* Stakeholders: Ɓangarorin da buƙatar ta shafa sosai
* Status: Matsayin buƙatar (daftari, an bita, an yi alƙawari, da sauransu)
* Owner: Mutumin da ke da alhakin aiwatar da buƙatar
* Author: Mutumin da ya rubuta buƙatar
* Revision: Lambar sigar bayanin
* Date: Ranar sabon bita
* Assumptions: Duk wani abu da zai iya haifar da matsala idan ba gaskiya ba ne yanzu ko daga baya
* Risks: Duk wani abu da zai iya haifar da lalacewa, jinkiri, ko wasu mummunan tasiri
* Defined: Ma’anar wata kalma (ya fi kyau a yi amfani da ƙamus)
