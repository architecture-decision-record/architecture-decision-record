# [000] Take
*Ba kowane ADR lamba don sauƙin nuni da jera shi* \
*LURA: Duk rubutun da aka ƙaga yana ba da alamu ne kuma ya kamata a cire shi kafin amfani na gaske*

## Matsayi - DAFTARI / MAI AIKI / AN DAINA AMFANI DA SHI ta [000] / YA MAYE GURBIN [000]

## Mahalli
*Bayyana a taƙaice matsalar(matsalolin) da wannan ADR ke son magancewa, da dalilin da ya sa matsalolin suke.*

## Hanyar da Aka Yanke
*Bayyana dalla-dalla shawarar da ke da muhimmanci ta fuskar gine-gine wadda aka yanke / za a yanke, kuma ku bayyana yadda take magance matsalolin da aka bayyana a sashen Mahalli.*

## Sakamako
*Menene tasirin wannan shawara a kan sifofin gine-gine da buƙatu masu aiki na tsarin?*

## Mulki
*Ta yaya za a sa ido kan sakamakon wannan shawara?* \
*Ta yaya za a tabbatar da bin wannan shawara?*

## Nazarin Zaɓuɓɓuka
*Idan ya dace, haɗa ko ku danganta duk wani nazarin musanya da aka yi don cimma shawarar da ke cikin wannan takarda.*

### Mabuɗi
*Na zaɓi: Ba masu ruwa da tsaki taimakon gani da zai taimaka musu su gane da sauri musanyar masu kyau da marasa kyau - misali haske mai sauƙi irin na fitilar zirga-zirga tare da prefix na mai kyau ko marar kyau.*

Bangon <span style="background-color:#4bce97; color:black;">kore</span> yana nuna dacewa mai kyau, yana ƙara muni ta <span style="background-color:#f1c232; color:black;">rawaya</span>, tare da <span style="background-color:#e06666; color:black;">ja</span> a matsayin mafi muni. \
\+ yana nuna sharhi mai tasiri mai kyau \
\- yana nuna sharhi mai tasiri marar kyau
