# Samfurin rikodin shawara don muhimman shawarwarin fasaha (ITD)

Wannan shi ne samfurin muhimman shawarwarin fasaha (ITD) da aka bayyana a
[ITDs: a lean ADR for executive technical decision-making at scale - Ignacio Larrañaga](https://ignaciolarranaga.medium.com/itds-a-lean-adr-for-executive-technical-decision-making-at-scale-e18bb3f6a563).

ITD ci gaba ne mai mai da hankali na ADR, wanda aka inganta don sauri, bayyanawa, da tabbatar da shugabanci. Inda ADR ke rubuta abin da aka yanke, ITD kayan aiki ne mai sauƙi, mai fara da shawara, wanda ke sa shawarar kanta ta zama mai yiwuwa a bita, domin masu ruwa da tsaki su iya dubawa da sauri kuma su ƙalubalance ta cikin sauƙi. ITD sun dace da shawarwarin fasaha waɗanda ba lallai ne na gine-gine ba, kamar zaɓar samfuri, laburare, ko dabarar CI/CD.

A cikin kowane fayil na ITD, rubuta waɗannan sassa:

# Take

Bayyana shawarar kanta, ba bayanin batun ba.
Misali, "Yi amfani da Qwen2.5 1.5B Instruct don fassara a cikin na’ura".

## Matsala

Jumla ɗaya da ke bayyana abin da muke ƙoƙarin warwarewa.

## Zaɓuɓɓukan da aka yi la’akari

Madadin da suka kasance a kan tebur, tare da zaɓin da aka zaɓa a cikin **rubutu mai kauri**.

## Dalili

Abubuwan da suka yanke hukunci ne kawai waɗanda suka kai ga zaɓin, ba cikakken jerin kowane fa’ida da rashin fa’ida ba.

## Bayanan kula

Na zaɓi. Duk wani ƙarin mahalli da ya cancanci a rubuta, kamar ƙuntatawa, zato, ko hanyoyin haɗi.
