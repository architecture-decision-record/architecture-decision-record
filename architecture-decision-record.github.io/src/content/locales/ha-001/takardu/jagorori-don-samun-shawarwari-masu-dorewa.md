# Jagorori don samun shawarwari masu dorewa

<https://www.infoq.com/articles/sustainable-architectural-design-decisions/>

Mun koyi waɗannan darussa a aikinmu waɗanda za su iya zama jagorori da kimantawa don samun shawarwari masu dorewa:

1. Yi amfani da hanya mai sauƙi/ƙaranci don rubuta shawarwari na farko.

2. Fifita kuma rubuta dukkan muhimman shawarwari waɗanda suka isa dacewa don rubutawa da fahimtar gine-ginen da ake nufi.

3. Cikakken bayani kan muhimman shawarwari da cikakkun samfura bayan an yi aikin farko ne kawai (wato, lokacin da masu yanke shawara suka gamsu da shawarwarin gine-gine da aka yanke kuma suka tabbata cewa ba sai an sake duba waɗannan shawarwari nan ba da jimawa ba).

4. Yi amfani da sigogin mataki na 1 masu sauƙi/ƙaranci a matsayin gajeren sigar shawarwarin da aka rubuta tare da matakin laushi da ya dace don ba da bayyani kan shawarwari dalla-dalla, da kuma shawarwari marasa muhimmanci ko bayyanannu.

5. Duk inda zai yiwu, yi amfani da ilimin gine-gine da ke akwai, ko daga samfuran jagora ko daga wasu tushe. Bita kuma faɗaɗa irin wannan ilimi kuma ku daidaita shi da mahallin shawarar ta musamman.

6. Tabbatar an kafa hanyoyin bin diddigi tsakanin shawarwari da duka buƙatu da ƙirar gine-gine/lamba.

7. Ba da duba daidaito ta atomatik don tabbatar da hanyoyin bin diddigi suna aiki tare bayan canji. Iyakance yawan dogaro tsakanin shawarwari da sauran kayayyakin manhaja.

8. Yi amfani da jagororin hujjoji a hankali kuma da ƙarfi—sune mafi muhimmin sashen rubuta shawara domin suna ba da dalili.
