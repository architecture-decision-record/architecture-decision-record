# Rikodin shawarar gine-gine don Google Cloud Platform

## Mahalli

Google Cloud Platform (GCP) sanannen dandamalin lissafin girgije ne wanda ke ba da ayyukan girgije daban-daban, ciki har da mafita na lissafi, ajiya, da sadarwa. Wannan ADR yana nufin rubuta shawarwarin gine-ginen da aka yanke don haɓakawa da aiwatar da kayayyakin more rayuwa na tushen GCP don ƙungiyarmu.

## Shawara

Ƙungiyarmu ta yanke shawarar amfani da Google Cloud Platform a matsayin kayayyakin more rayuwa na girgije don aikace-aikacenmu. Manyan la’akari na wannan shawara su ne:

   - Rashin tsada

   - Iya faɗaɗuwa

   - Aminci

   - Sassauci

## Zaɓaɓɓu

An zaɓi waɗannan ayyuka daga GCP don biyan buƙatunmu:

   - Compute Engine don injunan zahiri da albarkatun lissafi

   - Cloud Storage don ajiyar abubuwa da ɗaukar fayiloli

   - Cloud SQL don sabis ɗin rumbun bayanai da ake gudanarwa

   - Firebase don haɓaka da ɗaukar aikace-aikace

## Dalili

   - Rashin Tsada: Google Cloud Platform ba shi da tsada sosai idan aka kwatanta da sauran dandamalin girgije, wanda ya sa ya zama zaɓi mai jan hankali ga ƙungiyoyi masu ƙarancin kasafi.

   - Iya Faɗaɗuwa: Kayayyakin more rayuwa na GCP masu sauƙin faɗaɗawa suna ba da damar sarrafa kowane adadin zirga-zirga a lokaci-na-gaske.

   - Aminci: Ayyukan da GCP ke gudanarwa suna ba da babban aminci, tare da ajiyar madadin ta atomatik da iyawar murmurewa daga bala’i waɗanda ke tabbatar da samun albarkatu da bayanai sosai.

   - Sassauci: Dandamalin yana ba da kayan aiki da ayyuka daban-daban a fannoni kamar AI, nazarin bayanai, da IoT, wanda ya sa ya zama mai amfani da yawa.

## Sakamako

Komawa Google Cloud Platform zai buƙaci horar da tawagoginmu kan ayyukan GCP, sake tsara aikace-aikacen don ya dace da ayyukan da aka zaɓa, da sabunta lambar kayayyakin more rayuwa don tallafawa ayyukan GCP. Duk da haka, ana sa ran cewa da zarar an kammala komawar, za mu sami kayayyakin more rayuwa masu iya faɗaɗuwa sosai, masu aminci, da marasa tsada don ɗaukar aikace-aikacenmu. Haka kuma, za mu buƙaci sarrafa kuɗaɗen da ake kashewa akai-akai na samar da albarkatu a GCP.

## Kammalawa

Google Cloud Platform zaɓi ne mai kyau ga kayayyakin more rayuwanmu na girgije saboda rashin tsadarsa, iya faɗaɗuwarsa, amincinsa, da sassaucinsa. Ta amfani da ayyukan da aka zaɓa, za mu iya samar da kayayyakin more rayuwa da ake samu sosai kuma masu ƙarfi don aikace-aikacenmu.
