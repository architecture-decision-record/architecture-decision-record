# Decisioun record templat for Alexandrian pattren

## Introduction

* Prologue (Summary)
* Discussion (Context)
* Solution (Decision)
* Consequences (Results)

## Specifics ##

* Prologue (Summary)
  * Statement tae summarise:
    * In the context o (use case)<br>
      facin (concern)<br>
      we decidit for (option)<br>
      tae achieve (quality)<br>
      acceptin (downside).
* Discussion (Context)
  * Explains the forces at play (technical, political, social, project).
  * This is the story explainin the problem we are leukin tae resolve.
* Solution
  * Explains hoo the decisioun will solve the problem.
* Consequences
  * Explains the results o the decisioun ower the lang term.
  * Did it wark, no wark, wis chynged, upgradit, etc.
