# Decision record templet fo business case

Dis ADR templet dey emphasize to create business case for decision, including criteria, candidates, and costs.


## Top-level

* Title
* Status
* Evaluation criteria
* Candidates to consider
* Research and analysis of each candidate
  * Does/doesn't meet criteria and why
  * Cost analysis
  * SWOT analysis
  * Opinions and feedback
* Recommendation


## Low-level deep dive

**Title**:

  * Short present tense imperative phrase, less than 50 characters, like git commit message.

**Status**:

  * One of proposed, accepted, rejected, deprecated, superseded, etc.

**Evaluation criteria**:

  * Summary: explain briefly wetin we wan discover and why.

  * Specifics

**Candidates to consider**:

  * Summary: explain briefly how we discover di candidates, and draw attention to any outliers.

  * List all candidates and related options; wetin we dey evaluate as potential solutions?

  * Specifics

**Research and analysis of each candidate**:

  * Summary: explain briefly di research methods, and draw attention to patterns, clusters, and outliers.

  * Does/doesn't meet criteria and why

    * Summary

    * Specifics

  * Cost analysis

    * Summary

    * Examples

      * Licensing, like contract agreements and legal commitments

      * Training, like upskilling and change management

      * Operating, like support and maintenance

      * Metering, like bandwidth and CPU usage

  * SWOT analysis

    * Summary

    * Strengths

    * Weaknesses

    * Opportunities

    * Threats

  * Internal opinions and feedback

    * Summary

    * Examples

      * By di team, ideally write by di actual person

      * From other stakeholders

      * Quality attributes a.k.a. cross-functional requirements

  * External opinions and feedback

    * Summary

    * Who dey provide di opinion?

    * Which other candidates you consider?

    * Wetin you dey create?

      * Examples

        * B2B or B2C

        * external-facing or employee-only

        * desktop or mobile

        * pilot or production

        * monolith or microservices

    * How you evaluate di candidates?

    * Why you choose di winner?

    * Wetin dey happen since then?

      * Examples

        * How di winner dey perform?

        * Wetin % of real-world production user traffic dey flow through di winner?

        * Which kinds of integrations dey involved, like with continuous delivery pipelines, content management systems, analytics and metrics, etc.?

        * Since you know wetin you know now, wetin you go advise people to do different?

  * Anecdotes

**Recommendation**:

  * Summary

  * Specifics
