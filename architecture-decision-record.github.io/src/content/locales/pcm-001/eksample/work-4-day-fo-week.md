# Decision record fo 4-day work-week

Title: How to Implement 4-Day Work-week

## Context and Problem Statement

Our organization don dey consider to switch go 4-day workweek. Di current work schedule of 5 days per week, 8 hours per day, don cause low productivity, employee burnout, and high turnover rates. Di change wey dem propose wan increase employee satisfaction, engagement, and retention, and finally improve organizational performance.

## Decision

We don decide to implement 4-day workweek starting from di next quarter for all employees across di organization.

## Rationale

Dem base di decision on dis factors:

1. Employee feedback: Survey wey dem conduct among di employees show say dem want more flexible work schedule and better work-life balance. Di majority of di respondents show interest for 4-day workweek.

2. Research findings: Studies on companies wey don implement 4-day workweek don show increased employee productivity, reduced absenteeism, and improved work-life balance.

3. Competitive advantage: To offer 4-day workweek fit be attractive benefit to potential employees and e fit help retain talent wey we get already.

4. Organizational goals: Di switch to 4-day workweek align with our organizational goals to create healthier and more productive work environment.

## Implementation Plan

Dem go take dis steps to implement di 4-day workweek:

1. Communication: Dem go communicate di decision to all employees through company-wide email and meeting with team leaders.

2. Schedule: Di new schedule go be Monday to Thursday for full-time employees, with option of flexible schedules for part-time employees. Dem go extend work hours to 10 hours per day.

3. Policy updates: Dem go update human resources policies, like leave policies and benefits, so dem go align with di new schedule.

4. Training: Dem go train managers on how to manage teams under di new schedule and how to track employee performance and productivity well.

5. Evaluation: Dem go evaluate di new work schedule after six months to determine im impact on employee satisfaction, engagement, and retention.

## Anticipated outcomes

Dem expect say di implementation of 4-day workweek go produce dis outcomes:

1. Increased employee satisfaction, engagement, and retention.

2. Improved work-life balance for employees.

3. Increased productivity and reduced absenteeism.

4. More attractive employee benefit package and competitive advantage for di hiring market.

## Conclusion

We believe say di implementation of 4-day workweek go benefit our organization and our employees. We dey look forward to see di positive impact of dis decision on our employees and our overall organizational performance.
