# Decision record fo work from home

Title: Employees fit work from home

Decision Maker: Management Team

Decision Date: July 1, 2021

## Background

Because of di ongoing COVID-19 pandemic, many employees don dey work remotely from home. Dis arrangement don provide benefits like increased flexibility, improved work-life balance and decrease for how di virus dey spread. Plus, numerous employees don talk say dem go like continue to work from home even after di pandemic don end.

## Decision

After dem weigh di pros and cons of to allow employees work from home, di management team don decide to implement permanent work from home policy for eligible employees. Di policy apply to all full-time employees wey fit effectively perform their duties from home and wey don show success to work remotely during di pandemic.

Eligible employees fit choose to work from home up to three days per week, while dem go require in-office work for di remaining two days. To ensure effective communication and collaboration, dem go require regular check-ins with team members and managers. Plus, dem go provide employees with di necessary equipment and resources to perform their duties effectively from home.

Dem go review and revisit di implementation of dis policy regularly to make sure say e still benefit both di employees and di company.

## Reasoning

Di decision to continue to allow employees work from home base on several factors, including employee work-life balance, greater flexibility and freedom, improved productivity, and less exposure to di risk of infection. If di company implement flexible work model, e fit attract and retain top talent while e still provide positive company culture.

## Implications

Di implementation of di permanent work from home policy go get several implications, including di need for ongoing communication and coordination among team members, to adjust work processes and protocols so dem go accommodate remote workforce, and to ensure say all employees get access to di necessary equipment, tools and resources.

## Conclusion

Di decision to implement permanent work from home policy dey in line with di company values to promote healthy work-life balance, support employee well-being and promote positive company culture. We believe say dis decision go enable our organization to be more agile and better suited to adapt to changing business needs while e improve employee satisfaction and productivity.
