# Decisioun record templat for business case

This ADR templat emphasises makin a business case for a decisioun, includin criteria, candidates, an costs.


## Top-level

* Title
* Status
* Evaluation criteria
* Candidates tae consider
* Research an analysis o each candidate
  * Daes/doesna meet criteria an why
  * Cost analysis
  * SWOT analysis
  * Opinions an feedback
* Recommendation


## Law-level deep dive

**Title**:

  * A short present tense imperative phrase, less than 50 characters, like a git commit message.

**Status**:

  * Ane o proposed, accepted, rejected, deprecated, superseded, etc.

**Evaluation criteria**:

  * Summary: explain briefly whit we seek tae discover an why.

  * Specifics

**Candidates tae consider**:

  * Summary: explain briefly hoo we discoverit candidates, an draw attention tae onie outliers.

  * List aw candidates an relatit options; whit are we evaluatin as potential solutiouns?

  * Specifics

**Research an analysis o each candidate**:

  * Summary: explain briefly the research methods, an draw attention tae patterns, clusters, an outliers.

  * Daes/doesna meet criteria an why

    * Summary

    * Specifics

  * Cost analysis

    * Summary

    * Examples

      * Licensin, sic as contract agreements an legal commitments

      * Trainin, sic as upskillin an chynge management

      * Operatin, sic as support an maintenance

      * Meterin, sic as bandwidth an CPU uisage

  * SWOT analysis

    * Summary

    * Strengths

    * Weaknesses

    * Opportunities

    * Threats

  * Internal opinions an feedback

    * Summary

    * Examples

      * Bi the team, ideally written bi the actual person

      * Frae ither stakeholders

      * Quality attributes a.k.a. cross-functional requirements 

  * External opinions an feedback

    * Summary

    * Wha is providin the opinion?

    * Whit are ither candidates ye consideret?

    * Whit are ye makin? 

      * Examples

        * B2B or B2C

        * external-facin or employee-only

        * desktop or mobile

        * pilot or production

        * monolith or microservices

    * Hoo did ye evaluate the candidates?

    * Why did ye chuise the winner?

    * Whit is happenin since than?

      * Examples

        * Hoo is the winner performin?

        * Whit % o real-warld production user traffic is flowin throu the winner?

        * Whit kinds o integratiouns are involved, sic as wi continuous delivery pipelines, content management systems, analytics an metrics, etc.?

        * Kennin whit ye ken noo, whit wud ye advise fowk tae dae differently?

  * Anecdotes

**Recommendation**:

  * Summary

  * Specifics
