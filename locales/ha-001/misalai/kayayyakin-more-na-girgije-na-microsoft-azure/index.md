# Rikodin shawarar gine-gine: kayayyakin more rayuwa na girgije na Microsoft Azure" 

Taken Shawara: Karɓar Microsoft Azure a matsayin kayayyakin more rayuwa na girgije na ƙungiyarmu

Wanda Ya Yanke Shawara: Babban Jami’in Bayanai

Kwanan wata: 2021-10-15

Matsayi: An amince

## Tushe

Ƙungiyar tana shirin komawa daga kayayyakin more rayuwa na gargajiya na cikin gida zuwa kayayyakin more rayuwa na girgije. Don cimma hakan, ƙungiyar ta kimanta masu samar da sabis na girgije da yawa kamar Amazon Web Services (AWS), Google Cloud Platform (GCP), Microsoft Azure, da IBM Cloud. Kowane mai samarwa yana da nasa jerin siffofi, fa’idodi, da tsarin farashi. Bayan cikakken nazari, an kammala cewa Microsoft Azure shi ne zaɓi mafi dacewa ga ƙungiyarmu.

## Shawara

Karɓi Microsoft Azure a matsayin kayayyakin more rayuwa na girgije na ƙungiyarmu.

## Dalili

Waɗannan su ne dalilan karɓar Microsoft Azure:

1. Cikakkun Ayyuka: Azure yana ba da cikakken jerin ayyukan girgije daga kayayyakin more rayuwa a matsayin sabis (IaaS) zuwa manhaja a matsayin sabis (SaaS) da dandamali a matsayin sabis (PaaS). Wannan yana ba mu damar amfani da ayyukan Azure don warware buƙatun ƙungiyar, kamar ajiya, lissafi, sadarwa, da nazari.

2. Iya Faɗaɗuwa da Sassauci: Da Azure, za mu iya ƙara ko rage albarkatu cikin sauƙi bisa buƙatun kasuwancinmu. Azure kuma yana ba da sassauci wajen zaɓar tsarin aiki, harshen shirye-shirye, da tsarin aiki na manhaja.

3. Tsaro da Bin Ƙa’ida: Azure yana ba da babban matakin tsaro da bin ƙa’ida, domin yana bin ma’aunai da yawa na masana’antu kamar ISO, SOC, HIPAA, da PCI DSS. Azure kuma yana ba da siffofin tsaro na ci gaba kamar ƙungiyoyin tsaron hanyar sadarwa, firewalls, da kariyar DDoS.

4. Girgije Gauraye: Azure yana ba da ƙwarewar girgije gauraye mara matsala, yana ba ƙungiyoyi damar haɗa kayayyakin more rayuwarsu na cikin gida da Azure. Wannan yana sauƙaƙa mana mayar da ayyuka zuwa girgije kuma yana ba mu sassauci wajen zaɓar inda za a adana bayanai.

5. Rashin Tsada: Azure yana ba da tsarin farashi na biya-gwargwadon-amfani, wanda ke nufin muna biyan albarkatun da muka yi amfani da su kawai. Wannan yana taimaka mana mu tanadi kuɗi idan aka kwatanta da kayayyakin more rayuwa na gargajiya na cikin gida.

## Kammalawa

Karɓar Microsoft Azure a matsayin kayayyakin more rayuwa na girgije na ƙungiyarmu ya dace da buƙatun kasuwancinmu kuma yana ba da fa’idodi da yawa kamar iya faɗaɗuwa, sassauci, tsaro, da rashin tsada. Saboda haka, ana ba da shawarar karɓar Azure a matsayin kayayyakin more rayuwanmu na girgije.
