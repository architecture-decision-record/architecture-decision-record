# Samfurin rikodin shawara don tsarin Alexandrian

## Gabatarwa

* Gabatarwa (Taƙaitawa)
* Tattaunawa (Mahalli)
* Mafita (Shawara)
* Sakamako (Sakamakon ƙarshe)

## Cikakkun bayanai ##

* Gabatarwa (Taƙaitawa)
  * Bayani don taƙaitawa:
    * A cikin mahallin (yanayin amfani)<br>
      da fuskantar (damuwa)<br>
      mun yanke shawara don (zaɓi)<br>
      don cimma (inganci)<br>
      muna karɓar (rashin fa’ida).
* Tattaunawa (Mahalli)
  * Yana bayyana ƙarfin da ke aiki (fasaha, siyasa, zamantakewa, aiki).
  * Wannan labari ne da ke bayyana matsalar da muke neman warwarewa.
* Mafita
  * Yana bayyana yadda shawarar za ta warware matsalar.
* Sakamako
  * Yana bayyana sakamakon shawarar a dogon lokaci.
  * Ko ta yi aiki, ko ba ta yi ba, ko an canza ta, an inganta ta, da sauransu.
