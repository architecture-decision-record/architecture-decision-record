# Samfurin rikodin shawara na Michael Nygard

Wannan shi ne samfurin da ke cikin [Documenting architecture decisions - Michael Nygard](http://thinkrelevance.com/blog/2011/11/15/documenting-architecture-decisions).
Kuna iya amfani da [adr-tools](https://github.com/npryce/adr-tools) don sarrafa fayilolin ADR.

A cikin kowane fayil na ADR, rubuta waɗannan sassa:

# Take

Menene matsayin, kamar an ba da shawara, an karɓa, an ƙi, an daina amfani, an maye gurbinsa, da sauransu?

## Mahalli

Menene matsalar da muke gani wadda ke motsa wannan shawara ko canji?

## Shawara

Menene canjin da muke ba da shawara da/ko muke yi?

## Sakamako
