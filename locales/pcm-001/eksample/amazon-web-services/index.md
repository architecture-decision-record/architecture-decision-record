# Architecture Decision Record fo Amazon Web Services

Title: To Adopt Amazon Web Services fo Cloud Infrastructure

## Decision

After thorough evaluation of various cloud infrastructure platforms, we don decide say our organization go adopt Amazon Web Services (AWS) for our cloud infrastructure needs.

## Background

Our organization dey grow fast and we need reliable and scalable cloud infrastructure to meet di demands of our business. After we consider plenty cloud infrastructure providers, we conclude say AWS offer di most suitable range of services to meet our current and future requirements.

## Considerations

Plenty cloud infrastructure providers dey for market wey don prove say dem reliable and efficient. But AWS no only offer comprehensive set of services to cater to our needs, e still get extensive customer base and track record of successful deployments. AWS also provide broad range of support and training resources to make sure say our team fit work effectively with di platform.

Plus, AWS get extensive global presence with data centers for different regions around di world. Dis one ensure say our data secure and our applications and services go dey highly available. AWS also get robust security framework wey critical for our business.

Lastly, AWS provide pay-as-you-go model wey give us flexibility to scale our infrastructure based on demand. Dis mean say dem go only charge us for wetin we use, wey allow us optimize our costs and invest for other areas of di business.

## Decision

Based on di evaluations wey we perform and di advantages wey AWS offer, we don decide say we go adopt AWS for our cloud infrastructure needs.

## Consequences

To adopt AWS fit require training for our teams to make sure say dem familiar with di platform and fit use am effectively. E fit also get some initial costs to move our current infrastructure go di AWS platform.

## Ownership

Di cloud infrastructure team go dey responsible to make sure say dem use AWS effectively and to manage di risks wey associate with am. Dis team must address any issues wey arise.

## Review

Dem go review di decision to adopt AWS every year to make sure say e still meet our needs and say dem dey fully realize di benefits of di platform.
