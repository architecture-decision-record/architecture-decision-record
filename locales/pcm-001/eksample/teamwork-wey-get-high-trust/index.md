## Decision Description

Di decision to adopt High Trust Teamwork as core value and belief for our team and organizational culture.

## Alternatives Considered

We consider alternative approaches to teamwork, including low trust or no trust teamwork, and also other frameworks like cognitive diversity teams, centralized decision-making teams, and agile teams.

## Benefits and Risks

Di benefits of high trust teamwork include improved collaboration, better decision-making, increased motivation and engagement, higher job satisfaction and retention rates, and enhanced innovation and creativity. We acknowledge di importance of to build and maintain trust through transparency, honesty, integrity, and respect, and we recognize say dis approach go help us establish positive and rewarding work environment.
