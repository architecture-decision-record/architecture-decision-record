# Decision record templet wey dey use Planguage

Check https://www.iaria.org/conferences2012/filesICCGI12/Tutorial%20Specifying%20Effective%20Non-func.pdf

## Wetin be Planguage?

Planguage na planning language wey dey use dis keywords:

* Tag: Unique, persistent identifier
* Gist: Short summary of di requirement or area wey e address
* Requirement: Di text wey dey detail di requirement itself
* Rationale: Di reasoning wey justify di requirement
* Priority: Statement of priority and claim on resources
* Stakeholders: Parties wey di requirement materially affect
* Status: Di status of di requirement (draft, reviewed, committed, etc.)
* Owner: Di person wey responsible to implement di requirement
* Author: Di person wey write di requirement
* Revision: Version number for di statement
* Date: Di date of di most recent revision
* Assumptions: Anything wey fit cause problems if e no true now or later
* Risks: Anything wey fit cause malfunction, delay, or other negative impacts
* Defined: Di definition of one term (e better to use glossary)
