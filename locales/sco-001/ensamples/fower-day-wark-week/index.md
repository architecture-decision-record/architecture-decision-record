# Decisioun record for 4-day wark-week

Title: Implementation o 4-Day Wark-week

## Context an Problem Statement

Oor organisation haes been considerin a switch tae a 4-day warkweek. The current wark schedule o 5 days per week, 8 oors per day, haes resultit in laich productivity, employee burnout, an heich turnower rates. The proposed chynge aims tae increase employee satisfaction, engagement, an retention, an ultimately improve organisational performance.

## Decision

We hae decidit tae implement a 4-day warkweek stairtin frae the neist quarter for aw employees athort the organisation.

## Rationale

The decisioun wis based on the follaein factors:

1. Employee feedback: A survey conductit amang the employees heichlichtit the desire for a mair flexible wark schedule an a better wark-life balance. The majority o the respondents expressit interest in a 4-day warkweek.

2. Research findins: Studies on companies that hae implementit a 4-day warkweek hae shawn increased employee productivity, reduced absenteeism, an improved wark-life balance.

3. Competitive advantage: Offerin a 4-day warkweek can be an attractive benefit tae potential employees an can help retain existin talent.

4. Organisational goals: The switch tae a 4-day warkweek aligns wi oor organisational goals tae mak a healthier an mair productive wark environment.

## Implementation Plan

The follaein steps will be taen tae implement the 4-day warkweek:

1. Communication: The decisioun will be communicatit tae aw employees via a company-wide email an a meetin wi team leaders.

2. Schedule: The new schedule will be Monday tae Thursday for full-time employees, wi the option o flexible schedules for part-time employees. Wark oors will be extendit tae 10 oors per day.

3. Policy updates: Human resources policies, sic as leave policies an benefits, will be updatit tae align wi the new schedule.

4. Training: Managers will be trained on hoo tae manage teams unner the new schedule an hoo tae properly track employee performance an productivity.

5. Evaluation: The new wark schedule will be evaluatit efter sax months tae determine its impact on employee satisfaction, engagement, an retention.

## Anticipatit outcomes

The implementation o a 4-day warkweek is expectit tae result in the follaein outcomes:

1. Increased employee satisfaction, engagement, an retention.

2. Improved wark-life balance for employees.

3. Increased productivity an reduced absenteeism.

4. Mair attractive employee benefit package an a competitive advantage in the hirin market. 

## Conclusion

We believe that the implementation o a 4-day warkweek will benefit oor organisation an oor employees. We look forrit tae seein the positive impact o this decisioun on oor employees an oor owerall organisational performance.
